Systemic Bottleneck Analysis Slide 02 / 06

The Problem: Preventable Rejection Overhead

Applications fail DBT & scholarships not due to ineligibility, but due to preventable document defects.

Why Applications Get "Sent Back"

Thousands of rural students and welfare applicants face sudden scholarship rejections or DBT credit failures caused by silent document defects: income certificate validity lapses, phonetic name discrepancies across Aadhaar/Marksheet, and unlinked NPCI bank accounts.

The 100-Case Synthetic Technical Benchmark

In our technical evaluation across 100 synthetic government applications, standard state e-District portals blindly accepted PDFs with expired dates and mismatched surname spellings. The portal failed to validate at the operator counter, shifting the entire resolution burden onto backend human scrutiny clerks.

The Existing AI Fallacy
Chatbots only recite scheme rules. They cannot cross-audit physical uploaded documents.
Critical Gap
